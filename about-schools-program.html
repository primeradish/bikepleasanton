Bike Pleasanton / School Program

Every kid deserves a safe way to ride to school.

How our School Program Works

🧭 Map student routes

We collect the routes kids take to school through our school bike surveys and have families identify problem intersections and blocks.

🚦 Work with the city and schools to develop safety improvements

We bring that data to the city and schools to advocate for improvements that will create safer streets.

🤝 Organize the community

We work with parents and community members who want to bring the bike route survey or Safe Routes to School Programs to their school.

Why safer routes to school

Our members let us know that creating safer paths for kids to bike to school was their number one priority. Providing safe streets for kids to bike to school reduces school traffic, increases kids's autonomy, reduces greenhouse gas emissions, and improves the health of kids in Pleasanton.
